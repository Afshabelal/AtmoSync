## 2. Project Objective

The objective of AtmoSync is to analyze localized weather and micro-climate variations across nearby geographical locations and identify significant environmental differences that may indicate potential opportunities for further business analysis.

The project will use data analytics techniques including data collection, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, visualization, and dashboard development.

---

## 3. Business Problem

Weather conditions can vary significantly between nearby locations because of geographical and environmental factors.

AtmoSync aims to identify these localized variations and determine whether they create measurable opportunities that could be investigated further from a business perspective.

The environmental analysis itself does not represent guaranteed financial arbitrage. Any commercial opportunity would require additional business data such as energy prices, demand, logistics costs, or market prices.

---

## 4. Key Questions

1. How do weather conditions differ between nearby locations?
2. Which locations experience the largest temperature differences?
3. How does humidity vary across locations?
4. Are there significant differences during particular hours or days?
5. Which locations show recurring micro-climate patterns?
6. Can these differences be converted into an explainable opportunity score?
7. What business factors would be required to validate a real arbitrage opportunity?
